Why Free Range Farm?

Not one of our birds is kept in a cage. That is the whole of it — cage-free from the first day to the last, with the run of a house and the open ground beyond it. Free range is the English for keeping them that way, and it is where the farm takes its name.

How they are kept

Four Things That Make the Difference

Semi-Enclosed Living Space

Our free range chickens are raised within a semi-enclosed poultry house — an enclosed house joined to an outdoor activity space. The house gives them a well-equipped, shaded environment to grow in; the open ground lets them roam, forage and take the air. That freedom to move is what builds the chewier muscle texture and lower fat content you taste in the meat.

Room to Breathe

Animal wellbeing comes first here, so we do not crowd our birds into a coop the way commercial broiler farms do. Given enough space to breathe and move, the flock stays naturally healthier and avoids much of the disease that follows crowding.

Beside the Lake

The vast lake on our farm circulates freely, sourced straight from the surrounding mountains. Clean natural water, in its most natural form, for birds that drink from it every day.

A Feed We Mix Ourselves

Alongside everything they forage, our birds are fed a well-formulated feed we make on the farm. Foraged insects, seeds and plants give them the omega-3 and the lower saturated fat; the feed makes certain they get the full nutrition they need across a long, slow life. A balanced diet, not a gamble on the weather.
