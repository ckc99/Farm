free range chicken

We Promise

A bird that lives well simply tastes better. Room to roam, time to grow at its own pace and food it forages for itself build firmer muscle and a cleaner flavour — which is why we keep our flock the way we do.

  • Raised naturally

    A semi-enclosed house opening onto open ground, so the flock can shelter, roam and forage as it chooses.

  • Premium meat quality

    More than eighty days to grow, at the bird’s own pace. That longer time is what builds finer breast fibre and a firmer, juicier bite.

  • Free of antibiotics and artificial additives

    Birds given room and time stay healthier on their own, so there is nothing to prop up.

A bird free to go outdoors and forage for its own food, rather than living its whole life inside a shed — what most Malaysians call ayam kampung, or kampung chicken. Grown slowly rather than fast, which is what gives the meat its firmer, juicier bite.
