Packing & keeping

How It Reaches You

Vacuum-packed
Drawing the air out stops the meat oxidising, so it does not dull or taint in the freezer.
Blast-frozen
Freezing fast rather than slowly prevents freezer burn and keeps the juiciness and tenderness in the bird.
Keeping it
Refrigerate at 0°C to 4°C, or freeze below −18°C.
